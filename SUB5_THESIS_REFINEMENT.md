### Rationale for Changes

**DNN (-2%)**: Production timeline optimism warrants slight reduction. Still highest conviction holding given world-class assets, but 12-month delay probability is non-trivial.

**BBAI (-3%)**: Revenue declining despite acquisitions indicates execution challenges. Analyst target implying downside is a red flag. Reduce exposure until revenue stabilizes.

**URG (unchanged)**: Near-term catalyst (Shirley Basin Q1 2026) and unanimous Strong Buy ratings warrant maintaining position despite CEO transition risk. Upside/downside remains favorable.

**RR (-7%)**: Audit revealed accelerating revenue decline that contradicts "stabilization" narrative. Cannot maintain meaningful position in company with deteriorating fundamentals regardless of thematic appeal.

**SFET (-8%)**: Eliminated entirely. At $4M market cap, even 100% gain is immaterial to portfolio. Liquidity insufficient for exit. Redeploy capital to higher-conviction opportunities.

**Cash (+20%)**: Critical addition. Provides buffer for volatility, dry powder for opportunistic adds on pullbacks, and reduces overall portfolio risk. Can deploy when catalysts materialize or valuations improve further.
