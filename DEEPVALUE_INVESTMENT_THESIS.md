## Part I: The Deep Value Opportunity in Sub-$2 Stocks

### Market Inefficiency

Stocks trading below $2 experience systematic neglect from institutional investors due to:
- Minimum price thresholds at many funds
- Perceived "penny stock" stigma
- Limited analyst coverage
- Index exclusion

This creates mispricing opportunities where fundamentally sound companies trade at substantial discounts to intrinsic value. Unlike true penny stocks with no revenue or business model, deep value sub-$2 stocks possess:
- Identifiable revenue streams
- Manageable balance sheets
- Clear competitive positions
- Near-term re-rating catalysts

### Why Now?

Several factors make late 2025 particularly attractive for deep value micro-caps:
1. **Sector rotation**: Money flowing to mega-caps left small-caps at historic discounts
2. **Rising M&A**: Strategic acquirers seeking undervalued targets
3. **Rate cuts**: Lower cost of capital benefits high-beta names
4. **Tax loss selling**: November-December creates entry opportunities
