# DITG Investment Scope: Pre-Revenue Biotech Catalyst Portfolio

## Investment Theme

**Binary Event-Driven Biotech Investing (Q4 2025 - Q2 2026)**

This portfolio targets pre-revenue clinical-stage biotechnology companies with high-impact catalysts (FDA approvals, Phase 2/3 data readouts) expected within 3-9 months. These are binary event investments where successful catalysts can generate 200-500%+ returns while failures can result in 50-80% losses.

## Binary Event Framework

### Success Scenario (Per Holding)
- Positive Phase 2/3 data or FDA approval
- Stock appreciation: 200-500%+
- Duration: 1-3 days post-announcement

### Failure Scenario (Per Holding)
- Missed endpoints or clinical hold
- Stock decline: 50-80%
- Duration: Immediate

### Portfolio Approach
By holding 5 positions, portfolio diversifies binary risk. Even with 2 failures and 3 successes, expected portfolio return is positive.

---

## Selection Criteria

1. **Near-term catalysts**: Data readout or regulatory decision within 9 months
2. **Differentiated mechanism**: Novel approach to disease
3. **Clear endpoints**: Binary success/failure determination
4. **Depressed valuation**: Market pricing in low probability of success
5. **Orphan/rare disease focus**: Faster regulatory pathway, market exclusivity
