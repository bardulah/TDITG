# DITG Investment Scope: High-Risk/High-Reward Sub-$5 Multi-Sector Portfolio

## Investment Theme

**Strategic Micro-Cap Value Investing Across Emerging Technology and Critical Resources (2025-2030)**

This analysis focuses on stocks trading below $5 that represent exposure to transformative secular themes: artificial intelligence, nuclear energy renaissance, robotics/automation, and cybersecurity. These micro-cap and small-cap companies offer asymmetric risk/reward profiles with potential for substantial appreciation if their technologies achieve commercial scale.

## Risk Factors

### Systematic Risks
- **Liquidity Risk**: Micro-caps have wider bid-ask spreads and lower trading volumes
- **Dilution Risk**: Small companies often fund growth through equity issuance
- **Bankruptcy Risk**: Limited financial resources can lead to insolvency
- **Volatility Risk**: Price swings of 20-50% are common

### Company-Specific Risks
- **BBAI**: Government contract concentration, execution on acquisitions
- **DNN**: Uranium price volatility, permitting delays, capital requirements
- **URG**: Operational execution, commodity exposure, CEO transition
- **RR**: Competition from established robotics players, commercial adoption rates
- **SFET**: Micro-cap with limited resources, product commercialization risk
